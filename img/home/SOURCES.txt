Pre-wedding home plan exercise images
=====================================

All figures are from Open Exercise Illustrations, a CC0 (public domain)
set of start/end position drawings:

  https://github.com/hitmanrepo/open-exercise-illustrations
  License: CC0 1.0 Universal — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Each source frame was downloaded, visually checked to confirm it shows
the named exercise, then cropped and composited locally into the JPEGs
used on the page. No images were generated for this page.
